How to play

Rock beats scissors.

Paper beats rock.

Scissors beats paper.

Same move? It's a draw. Best of 3, 5, or 7 means first to 2, 3, or 4 wins. Draws don't count toward the target.

In free play, stop whenever you like. End a session to save it. The computer picks randomly before you choose.
