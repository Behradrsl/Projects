THE 30-SECOND VERSION

Keep it simple.

Pick rock, paper, or scissors. The computer commits to a random move before you choose. Neither of you gets a peek.

  • Rock crushes scissors.
  • Scissors cuts paper.
  • Paper covers rock.

Best of 3 means first to 2 wins. Best of 5 needs 3; best of 7 needs 4. Draws don't advance the score, so a match can take more rounds than its name suggests.

In free play, keep going as long as you like. Use End this match to save the session. Ending a best-of match early records it as unfinished.

Use R, P, or S to play with your keyboard. History stays in your local Python server's database. Closing the tab leaves the current match available until the server restarts or it expires after 24 hours of inactivity.
